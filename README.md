# My Favorite Movie: I'm Drunk, I Love You

**I'm Drunk, I Love You* is a Filipino romantic comedy-drama film. Set days before their college graduation, it follows longtime best friends **Carson** (Maja Salvador) and **Dio** (Paulo Avelino) on one last road trip to La Union, where Carson finally decides to confess her seven-year unrequited feelings.

## Why I Love It
* **Painfully Relatable:** Captures the raw, messy reality of unrequited love and long-term friendships.
* **Iconic Soundtrack:** Features unforgettable OPM tracks like *"Burnout"* by 3D (Dancel, Dumas, Danao) and *"Lloydy"* by Shirebound and Busking.
* **Natural Chemistry:** Maja Salvador and Paulo Avelino make their friendship feel completely authentic.
